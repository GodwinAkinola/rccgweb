WHO WE ARE

About RCCG Praise Sanctuary

RCCG Praise Sanctuary exists to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ. We are a community committed to worship, discipleship, and compassionate service.

Our History

Founded to serve the local community, our church has grown through faithful teaching, worship, and outreach. We celebrate every life changed through the power of the Gospel.

Vision & Mission

Vision: A community transformed by God's love.

Mission: Teach the Word, love the people, serve the city.

Our Values

  • Worship — Jesus-centered praise and adoration
  • Community — Loving and supporting one another
  • Discipleship — Growing in the Word and prayer
  • Service — Reaching out to our neighbours

We host weekly services, prayer meetings, and community outreach programs. Everyone is welcome — whether you're exploring faith for the first time or want to grow deeper. Our ministries include youth, women's groups, children's ministry, and a counselling team.
